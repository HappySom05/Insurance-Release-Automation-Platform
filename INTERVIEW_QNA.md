Q. What is the objective of this project?

A. To automate software release activities using Jenkins, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform and Shell Scripting.

Q. Why Jenkins?

A. To automate build, test and deployment workflows.

Q. Why Docker?

A. To package applications and dependencies into portable containers.

Q. Why Kubernetes?

A. To orchestrate and manage containerized workloads.

Q. Why Terraform?

A. To provision infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code principles.
